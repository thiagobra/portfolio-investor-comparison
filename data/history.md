# Historical episodes

Peak-to-trough declines on a closing basis, and how long the index took to make a new nominal high.
"Belief" is the thing people actually believed on the way in — that is the part a portfolio screen
never shows you.

## Episodes

| Episode | Period | Drawdown | Recovery | Tags | Belief |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dot-com crash (Nasdaq Composite) | 2000-03 to 2002-10 | -78% | ~15 years, new high 2015-04 | Information Technology, Communication Services, Concentration | The internet changes everything, so the old valuation rules do not apply here. |
| Cisco Systems, the "safe" tech bet of 1999 | 2000-03 to 2002-10 | ~-90% | ~21 years, new high 2021-11 | Information Technology, Single stock | It is the profitable market leader, so it cannot be a bubble stock. |
| Japan's Nikkei 225 | 1989-12 to 2009-03 | -82% | ~34 years, new high 2024-02 | Home country, Whole market, Financials, Real Estate | My home market is the one I actually understand. |
| Global Financial Crisis (S&P 500) | 2007-10 to 2009-03 | -57% | ~5.5 years, new high 2013-03 | Whole market, Concentration, Industrials, Consumer Discretionary, Financials | Spread across 500 companies means genuinely diversified. |
| US property and REITs (FTSE Nareit Equity REITs) | 2007-02 to 2009-03 | ~-70% | ~7 years | Real Estate | House prices have never fallen nationwide. |
| Oil after the 1970s energy boom (WTI crude) | 1981 to 1986 | ~-70% nominal | ~14 years nominal | Energy, Materials | Energy is the largest sector in the index for a good reason. It was about 28% of the S&P 500 in 1980 and under 6% by 1999. |
| Gold after the 1980 peak (London PM fix) | 1980-01 to 1999-07 | -70% nominal | ~27 years nominal, new high 2007 | Gold | Gold protects you because it always holds its value. |
| Nifty Fifty unwind (S&P 500) | 1973-01 to 1974-10 | -48% | ~7.5 years | Consumer Staples, Health Care, Concentration | Great companies are worth buying at any price. |
| "Defensive" utilities in 2008 (S&P 500 Utilities, calendar-year total return) | 2008 calendar year | -29% | ~4 years | Utilities, Consumer Staples | Defensive sectors do not fall. They fell less than the market, but they still fell. |
| China and Hong Kong equities (Hang Seng) | 2021-02 to 2022-10 | -52% | not yet recovered | Emerging markets, Communication Services | A growing economy means a growing stock market. |
| Bitcoin, twice (daily closes) | 2017-12 to 2018-12, then 2021-11 to 2022-11 | -84% then -77% | ~3 years each | Crypto | The asymmetry is worth it and the drawdowns are just noise. |
| Bonds in 2022 (Bloomberg US Aggregate, total return) | 2022-01 to 2022-12 | -13.01%, worst calendar year since the index began in 1976 | not yet recovered | Fixed income | Bonds are the safe half of the portfolio. |
| COVID crash (S&P 500) | 2020-02-19 to 2020-03-23 | -34% in 33 days | ~5 months, new high 2020-08 | Whole market | I will have time to react when things start turning. |
| The Great Crash (Dow Jones Industrial Average) | 1929-09 to 1932-07 | -89% | ~25 years, new high 1954-11 | Whole market, Concentration | Stocks always come back, you just have to wait it out. |
