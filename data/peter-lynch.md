## Key Principles
- Invest in what you know and understand
- Look for GARP: Growth At a Reasonable Price
- Hunt for tenbaggers in underfollowed small/mid caps
- The PEG ratio matters more than the P/E alone
- Do your homework: read annual reports, visit stores
- Retail investors have real edges over Wall Street
